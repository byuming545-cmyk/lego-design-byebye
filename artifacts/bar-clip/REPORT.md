## Grammar and joint

- Profile: `ldcad-source-declared-round-bar-clip-r4-v1`.
- Insertion interval: `[1, 8]` LDU.
- Rotation: round/round, source limits `[0, 360]`; the native graph uses the
  conservative discrete keys `[0, 90, 180, 270]`.
- Slide: OR across the endpoints because either source side may declare
  `slide=true`; an absent clip `slide` is "not declared", not a positive
  permission.
- Joint: `CYLINDRICAL` when one side declares sliding and both establish roll.
- Evidence tier: `SOURCE_DECLARED_FIT`; not `VERIFIED_FIT`.
- `physicalBuildVerified: false`; friction, retention and stress remain `UNKNOWN`.

The negative control `2412b` (unnamed male R4 cylinder) stays `GENERIC_SPECIAL` and
does not become a BAR endpoint.

## Open-surface collision proof

`48729b` and `60470b` have genuinely open clip shells, so the closed-solid proxy
cannot represent them. They now build a **`open-source-surface`** proxy: all source
triangles are retained, no cap or signed volume is invented, and the topology
records `signedVolume: null`, `internalCaps: 0`.

`deriveNativePairCollision` accepts an open-surface pair only for a validated
source-declared `BAR_CLIP` seat. Every detected contact must lie inside the
R4 / 8-LDU cylindrical seat (with 0.002 LDU coordinate quantization), and no open
vertex may fall inside a closed counterpart. The proof explicitly keeps
`openSurfaceSeatChecked: true`, `completeSurfacePartition: false` and
`containmentChecked: false`. This is pose-clearance evidence, not a closed solid,
not a physical fit, and it does not weaken the existing closed-solid path.

Live collision result for the generated model: `30374` into `48729b` clip is
`clear` / `open-surface-seat-clearance` at all four native twists (1,314 triangle
tests per twist).

`60470b` is collision-ready now, but the 80-LDU `30374` bar intersects the plate
body at every quarter turn. That is a real geometric exclusion, not a proxy
blocker. The normal generator therefore selects `48729b` for the produced model.
No source-backed shorter standalone bar exists in the pinned library.
